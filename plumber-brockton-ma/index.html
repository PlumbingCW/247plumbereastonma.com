Campello, Montello and downtown: where calls come from

Brockton is a city of about 21 square miles, and its villages each have a different feel. Campello is the smallest neighborhood by area but the most populous, with its own commuter rail station and post office. Montello has its own station and post office too. Dense blocks like these mean shared walls, stacked bathrooms and one drain stack serving more than one apartment.

That layout changes how we handle an emergency. If a tenant on the second floor reports a backup, the first question is whether the first-floor unit or the basement is affected too. Tell the dispatcher how many units share the line and where the cleanout is, and the plumber arrives with the right cable and a plan.

Downtown and the Main Street corridor

Downtown Brockton has older commercial blocks with apartments or storefronts above and below each other. Plumbing in buildings like these often runs through shared basements and mixed-era pipe, so a leak in one unit can drip into the next. Shut off the water at the nearest valve and call the 24/7 line, then let us know if a business below is affected.

Before the plumber reaches your Brockton address

A few minutes of preparation saves time on arrival, especially in a building with several apartments.

Find the main shutoff

In a multi-family building there may be one main valve in the basement and separate valves for each unit. Know which one controls the leak.

Clear a path to the problem

Move stored boxes, laundry and furniture away from the basement drain, water heater or cleanout so the plumber can work right away.

Tell neighbors and the landlord

If the building has other tenants, let them know to stop using water. A single flush upstairs can push more sewage into a backed-up line.

Note which fixtures are affected

Write down which sinks, tubs and toilets are slow or backing up. That points toward a branch line or the main drain.

Photograph any damage

Take quick photos of standing water or wet walls before cleanup for your insurer or landlord.

Secure pets and open access

Keep animals away from the work area and make sure the basement door and any gate are open.

If water is near outlets or the electrical panel, stay out of the room and say so on the phone. That is an emergency we will treat first.

Triple-deckers, two-families and what they mean for your pipes

Brockton has an unusually high share of small multi-unit housing. A city housing strategy plan counted about 27.7 percent of homes in buildings of two to four units, and noted that Brockton's three-deckers are commonly freestanding next to lower-rise houses. About 45 percent of occupied homes are rented, which means a plumbing problem often involves a tenant, a landlord and a shared line.

Practically, that stacked layout concentrates fixtures on one drain stack. When it clogs, the backup tends to appear at the lowest unit first, often in a basement or first-floor tub. A tenant on the third floor may see only a slow drain while the first floor is already flooding, so a call about a slow drain upstairs can be a real emergency below.

Water heaters and shared systems

In a building with several apartments, each unit may have its own tank or share one. When a tank fails in a basement closet, water heater repair or water heater replacement is usually the next step. Turn off the power or gas to the unit and the cold supply valve, then call us.

Where Brockton's water and sewer come from

According to the City of Brockton water department, the main supply is the Silver Lake reservoir, with the Brockton Reservoir in Avon as a secondary source. A desalination plant in Dighton owned by Aquaria is the third source. The city's water division lists 24-hour emergency service for the system, so a broken main in the street is a call to them, while a break inside your walls is ours.

Wastewater goes to the Advanced Water Reclamation Facility on Oak Hill Way. If sewage backs up in your basement and neighbors report the same, it may be a city line issue and worth reporting. If only your building is affected, sewer line cleaning or drain cleaning usually clears the stoppage.
