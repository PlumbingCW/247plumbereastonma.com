Easton and its villages

Easton is a town in Bristol County, and its villages include North Easton, South Easton, Easton Center, Furnace Village, Eastondale and Five Corners. North Easton is home to the Ames civic buildings and the H. H. Richardson Historic District, while South Easton sits along the Route 138 corridor to the south.

Our address on Washington Street in North Easton is the center of the service area. From there, Route 138 runs north toward Stoughton and south toward Raynham and Taunton, Route 106 runs west toward Mansfield, and Route 123 leads toward Norton and Brockton.

Nearby towns with their own pages

Stoughton borders Easton to the north, and Sharon borders it to the northwest. Mansfield is the western neighbor, Norton sits to the southwest, and Brockton is the large city to the east.

Each page covers that town's housing, water supply and the calls that come up most, so you are not reading the same text with a different name.

Places we mention but do not list separately

Taunton, Raynham and West Bridgewater also border Easton. We can often reach addresses there, but they do not have dedicated pages yet. Call and the dispatcher will confirm whether a plumber can get to you.

ZIP codes in the core Easton area

The core Easton service area covers ZIP codes 02356 for North Easton, 02375 for South Easton and 02357 for the Stonehill College area, along with the 02334 ZIP that also carries the Easton name. If you are not sure which one is yours, check the address on a utility bill and read it to the dispatcher.

Nearby towns carry their own ZIP codes: 02072 for Stoughton, 02048 for Mansfield, 02301 and 02302 for Brockton, 02067 for Sharon and 02766 for Norton. Each town page lists its codes.

How dispatch decides who goes where

Dispatch looks at what you describe first and distance second. A burst pipe or a sewage backup moves ahead of a slow drain, because water damage grows by the minute. A planned job, such as a water heater installation, is scheduled around those calls.

Roads matter too. Route 138 carries most of the north and south traffic through Easton, while Routes 106 and 123 handle the east and west runs. In winter, a storm that closes a stretch of any of them changes what a realistic arrival looks like, which is why we do not promise a fixed time.

What to expect in each nearby town

Sewer and water setups differ from town to town. Parts of Norton and Sharon rely on private septic systems, so a backup there needs a different first question than it does in a sewered part of Brockton. Mansfield and Stoughton have their own mix of older village streets and newer development, and the plumber who comes will ask which you have.

Brockton, as a city, has many two and three family buildings, so a leak in one unit can reach another. If that is your situation, tell the dispatcher how many units share the line.

Where the area facts come from

According to MassDEP guidelines and each town's own water reports, supply and sewer setups vary across these towns. The state plumbing code, 248 CMR, applies everywhere.

What to do before you call from any of these towns

Before you call

  • Find your main shutoff valve if you can do so safely.
  • Note whether you are on town sewer or septic.
  • Tell the dispatcher if anyone in the home is elderly or has medical needs.
