Emergency Water Line Repair in Easton, MA

Your water service line is the pipe that carries town water from the street to your house. When it fails, the symptoms range from a soggy patch of lawn to no water at all, and the repair starts with knowing exactly where the break is.

What the water line is and where it runs

The service line starts at the water main under the street, passes a shutoff and the meter, and enters your house through the foundation or slab. Most of its length is buried, which is why a break can hide for weeks. It is not the same thing as the plumbing inside your walls, which is a separate job, usually a question for repiping and not a service line repair.

Easton's water comes from the Easton Water Division, a town department. Where the Division's responsibility ends and yours begins is something to confirm with them before anyone digs. The answer can depend on the location of the shutoff and the meter, and we would rather you hear it from the owner of the system than from us.

Signs of a water line emergency

A service line problem looks different from a leaking faucet. Watch for these.

Common symptoms

  • Wet, soft or unusually green patches of lawn between the street and the house
  • Water pressure that drops at every fixture, not just one
  • A meter that runs when every tap in the house is off
  • Muddy or discolored water after a quiet period
  • A hissing sound near the meter or where the line enters the foundation
  • No water at all after a freeze or nearby excavation

One of these on its own can have other causes. Two or three together make a failing service line the first thing to rule out.

What to do first when the line fails

1

Shut off the main valve

Close the valve inside the house, usually near the meter. If you cannot close it or it will not hold, say so when you call.

2

Check the meter

If the meter keeps moving after the main is closed, the loss is upstream of the valve, on the street side.

3

Avoid digging on your own

Underground lines, gas and electric services can share the same corridor. Call 811 before any excavation.

Why service lines fail in this part of Massachusetts

Frozen ground is the obvious culprit. A line laid too shallow, or one that runs under a driveway where snow is cleared and frost goes deeper, can crack in a hard winter. The break often shows up at the thaw when pressure returns.

Age matters as well. If the leak turns out to be inside the house instead, leak detection finds it. Older homes near North Easton may still have galvanized steel or early copper in the ground, and both corrode from the inside. Plastic lines in newer subdivisions fail more often at fittings than along the pipe.

Permits, utility locates and the Division

Massachusetts requires a permit from the local plumbing inspector before plumbing is installed, altered, removed, replaced or repaired, and only licensed plumbers can pull it. Ask the Easton building department what it needs for service line work, and note that we also dispatch to Norton and other nearby towns. Digging calls for a free utility locate through Dig Safe, reachable by dialing 811.

The Easton Water Division controls the main and the meter, so expect to coordinate on any shutoff at the street. A plumber who has handled these jobs knows to ask before turning anything off. If lead or old galvanized pipe turns up, the Division can tell you what testing or replacement programs apply.
