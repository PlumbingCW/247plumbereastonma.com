What to do first when a pipe bursts

Close the main water shutoff. It is often in the basement near where the water line enters, or beside the meter, and it turns clockwise to close. Then open a faucet at the lowest level to drain what is left in the pipes, which also lowers the pressure pushing water out of the break.

Switch off the breaker for any room where water is touching outlets, appliances or a ceiling light, and stay out of standing water if you cannot be sure the power is off. Move rugs and valuables, and call the 24/7 line. Take photos of the damage before you start cleaning, because your insurer will want them.

The first fifteen minutes, in order

1

Turn the house main valve fully closed. If it is stuck, say so on the phone.

2

Use buckets, towels and a wet vac for what you can, and protect valuables.

5

With the supply closed, power or gas to the heater should be off to protect it from running dry.

6

Call and photograph

Call us, then photograph damage for your insurer.

Frozen pipes: thawing, bursting and repair

Water expands as it freezes, and the pressure builds between the ice blockage and a closed faucet. The pipe usually splits where the ice sits, but you often do not see the leak until it thaws. A frozen pipe that has not yet cracked can be thawed carefully, while a split pipe needs a repair.

If a faucet gives no water in a cold snap, leave it open, shut the main as a precaution, and warm the area with a hair dryer or a space heater placed safely away from anything flammable. Never use an open flame or a torch. If you cannot find the frozen section or see any wet drywall, call us before the pipe thaws on its own.

Where pipes freeze and burst in Easton homes

New England cold snaps from December to February expose the same weak points in house after house.

Exterior walls

Pipes on the cold side of insulation, especially behind kitchen sinks on outside walls, are common freeze spots.

Crawlspaces and basements

Unheated crawlspaces, rim joist areas and pipes near a basement window or vent can drop below freezing.

Hose bibs and garages

An outdoor spigot that was not drained, or lines through an unheated garage, split at the first deep freeze.

Old galvanized pipe

Corroded steel lines in older houses have thin walls and fail under stress that new copper would survive.

Water hammer and pressure spikes

Loose pipes and sudden valve closures stress fittings over years until one gives.

Failed joints and fittings

Not every burst is a freeze. A tired coupling or a soldered joint can fail without any cold at all.

After the pipe is fixed: drying, damage and what comes next

Stopping the water is half the job. Wet drywall, insulation and flooring need to dry quickly, often within a day or two, to prevent mold. A water mitigation company and your insurer take over from there, and a plumber's written note on the cause helps your claim.

If a main or service line failed outside the house, see water line repair. If you suspect a slow leak you have not found yet, leak detection locates it without opening walls unnecessarily. We also dispatch to Norton and Mansfield when pipes burst there.

Permits and licensed work in Massachusetts

Repairing a leak in a faucet or valve is exempt from the permit requirement under 248 CMR 3.05. Replacing a section of pipe is not, so a burst pipe repair generally needs a permit pulled by a licensed plumber. In an emergency the regulations allow temporary use once the work is tested, if the inspector is told and inspects by the next working day.

That is another reason to use a licensed professional, not a handyman. You can check any plumber on the state's online license lookup, and the Easton building department can tell you how local inspections are handled.

Rules behind emergency pipe repair

According to the Massachusetts plumbing code, 248 CMR 3.05, a licensed plumber may start emergency work and then notify the local plumbing inspector. That is why a burst pipe is handled first and the paperwork follows.

Your insurer has its own guidelines for water damage claims, so photograph everything before you clean up.
