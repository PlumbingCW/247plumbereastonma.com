Using this website

This website provides general information about plumbing and about Easton 24/7 Plumbing. It is not a substitute for a licensed plumber's inspection of your property.

Services and pricing

Plumbing is performed by licensed plumbers dispatched through our phone line. You receive a clear price before work starts and you may decline. Prices on a call depend on the job, parts and time of day.

We do not promise a specific arrival time, because traffic, weather and call volume vary.

Information on this site

We try to keep local and legal information accurate, but codes, permits and utility details change. Confirm requirements with the Easton building department, the Easton Water Division or the Massachusetts Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters.

Calls and recordings

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By calling you agree to this.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for decisions made from the general information on this site. In an emergency, call 911 for gas odors, fire or danger to life before calling a plumber.
